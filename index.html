About this data

Covers transactions from 9 May 2024 through 25 Aug 2026. "Season" follows Ndugu's coffee-year convention rather than the calendar year, so a season typically runs from one year's harvest into the next. District is taken from each farmer's registered location. The map plots farmers whose registered coordinates fall inside the mapped area; records without a usable coordinate are included in every total but are not plotted. Age bands are derived from recorded dates of birth and exclude records where that date falls outside a plausible range. USD figures use an indicative rate of about 3,777 UGX per USD and are not a live conversion. Every chart and total on this page is computed over the complete dataset of 21,734 transactions.

Definitions

FAQ: Fair Average Quality. Certification: Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade and 4C. Active supplier: a registered farmer with at least one recorded delivery in this window. Batch: a bulked, graded lot dispatched to a buyer.
