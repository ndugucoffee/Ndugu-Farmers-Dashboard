Definitions

FAQ: Fair Average Quality, the ungraded green coffee bought from suppliers. Screen: bean size measured over a perforated sieve, so screen 18 is a larger bean than screen 12. Lot: a graded, bulked consignment shipped against one contract. Net delivered: weight after moisture and foreign-matter deductions.
