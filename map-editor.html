Click a plot to select it and show its vertices.

Drag a square handle to move that corner. Drag the middle of the plot to move the whole boundary. Drag a hollow handle to insert a corner.

Alt-click or right-click a square handle to delete that corner. A ring needs at least three.

Esc deselects.

Open file…, or drop files anywhere on the page: GeoJSON, KML/KMZ, shapefile (.zip, or .shp + .dbf + .prj together), GeoPackage, GPX, CSV/Excel with a WKT or lat/lng column, WKT, TopoJSON. Files are read in this browser and are not uploaded anywhere.

Screen for deforestation sends these boundaries to the screening tool running on this computer and colours each plot by what the satellite record shows, with the mapped 2020 forest and any loss since drawn underneath. It needs serve-dashboards.bat running and this page opened from 127.0.0.1; on the published site the button explains that and does nothing else. Results are a screening, not a finding of illegality, and they never leave this machine.
