About us

About us

Coffee, traced back to the farmer who grew it.

Ndugu Farmers Ltd buys FAQ-grade robusta from smallholder cooperatives across ten districts in Uganda's Greater Masaka region, bulks it into export-ready lots, and ships it to buyers who need to know exactly where it came from. The dashboards on this site are not a brochure: they are the same reporting the business runs on, published as it is.

How the trade works

From farm gate to export

Six steps, the same six every season.

Step 1

Registration

A farmer joins through a cooperative in one of ten districts. Their record (name, plot, district) starts here and follows the coffee for as long as they keep delivering.

Step 2

Delivery

Cherry and parchment are weighed and paid for at the buying site, on the day, and logged against the farmer who brought it in.

Step 3

Intake testing

Coffee Minds checks moisture and defect levels against Ndugu's standard as each delivery arrives, before it's bulked with anything else.

Step 4

Bulking & grading

Deliveries are bulked into lots and graded by screen size, ready to be matched against a buyer's specification.

Step 5

Export testing

Coffee Minds runs a final cupping and moisture check on the graded lot, confirmed against certification and geolocation requirements, before it's offered to a buyer.

Step 6

Export

Graded lots ship to UGACOF, ETG and other buyers, with the record (farmer, plot, certification, delivery date) intact behind every bag.
