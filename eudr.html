What the regulation asks for

-

-

Geolocation of every plot

Latitude and longitude for each plot the coffee was produced on, to at least six decimal digits. Fewer digits is not a rounding preference, it fails the test.

Art. 9(1)(d)

Polygons above 4 hectares

A plot larger than four hectares must be given as a polygon tracing its perimeter. A single point is only accepted at four hectares or below.

Art. 2(28)

Deforestation-free

The land must not have been converted from forest after 31 December 2020. The geolocation is what makes that checkable against satellite records.

Art. 3(a)

Legally produced

Production must comply with Uganda's law on land use, tenure and labour. Evidencing that needs the administrative location, not just the district.

Definitions

EUDR: Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 on deforestation-free products, as amended. Operator: the party first placing goods on the EU market. DDS: the due diligence statement an operator files before placing a consignment. Plot: a single parcel of land on which the coffee was produced. Polygon: the traced perimeter of a plot, as opposed to a single point inside it.
